## About

**Section title:** The Golden Hour Has a Home

**Body:**
On Da Rocks sits right above Kanke Dam — which, honestly, does half the work for us. You get here as the sky starts turning, find a spot on the deck, order something off the grill, and just... let the evening happen. That's the whole idea.

We're not fine dining and we're not trying to be. We're the place your group chat picks when someone says "let's just go somewhere nice tonight" — good food, a proper bar, live music some evenings, and a view that genuinely doesn't get old, no matter how many times you've been.

**Callout stat line:** 350+ happy evenings and counting · Open till midnight, every day

## Signature Experience (4 pillars)

**01 — The View**
Perched right above Kanke Dam. Come for sunset, stay for the way the water looks once the lights come on.

**02 — The Grill**
Tandoor classics, Chinese favourites, and a full bar — the Paneer Tikka and Chicken Tikka are the ones people keep coming back for.

**03 — The Vibe**
Live music evenings, a crowd that's here to have a good time, and a space built for groups, not solo scrolling.

**04 — The Space**
50 to 200 guests, indoor or out on the deck. Birthdays, get-togethers, corporate nights — we've hosted them all.
